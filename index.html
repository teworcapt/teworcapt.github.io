My Work

PlayDok

As a child with absent parents, you're off to your normal play time of pretend. With a lack of siblings or friends to keep company, your childish mind comes to life as you stimulate a hospital with your crayons and stuffed toys. With your parent's Christmas gift to you, you use your testing tools and syringes to take tests and formulate diagnosis for your ill patients.
