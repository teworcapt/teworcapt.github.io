About Me

Hello! I am tewo, an artist and game developer with a passion and purpose of bringing stories and advocacies to life. I have been practicing art for most of my life while also dabbling in programming in my free time. My love for research and creative writing paired with my talent in art and programming has led me to taking game development as my program.

As a fourth year college student, I hope to master the fundamentals of art, literature, and programming, in hopes of creating something as deep as the games and media I have experienced. I am in a pursuit of creating meaningful media while also pushing my advocacies for sea life and the environment.
